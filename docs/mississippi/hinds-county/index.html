Land and home values in Hinds County, Mississippi

Hinds County (metro core, Jackson, MS) covers 870 square miles and 67 census tracts. Residential land is about $1.61 per sq ft at the county median, from $0.69 in its most rural tracts to $7.11 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $133,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,300 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Hinds County, MS?

Residential land in Hinds County, MS is about $1.61 per square foot, or $70,306 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $17,576. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Hinds County, MS?

The typical (median) home in Hinds County, MS is worth about $133,000, with entry-level homes around $94,100 and upper-end homes around $210,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Hinds County, MS?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,300 a month in Hinds County, MS, a 1-bedroom about $1,100 and a 3-bedroom about $1,550 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Hinds County, MS?

Farmland averages about $3,910 an acre in Hinds County, MS (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
