Land and home values in Panola County, Mississippi

Panola County (rural) covers 685 square miles and 11 census tracts. Residential land is about $1.43 per sq ft at the county median, from $0.59 in its most rural tracts to $3.07 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $142,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,220 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Panola County, MS?

Residential land in Panola County, MS is about $1.43 per square foot, or $62,073 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $15,518. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Panola County, MS?

The typical (median) home in Panola County, MS is worth about $142,000, with entry-level homes around $66,700 and upper-end homes around $272,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Panola County, MS?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,220 a month in Panola County, MS, a 1-bedroom about $1,030 and a 3-bedroom about $1,530 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Panola County, MS?

Farmland averages about $3,540 an acre in Panola County, MS (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
