Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Mississippi?

Residential land in Mississippi is about $2.15 per square foot, or $93,785 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $23,446. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Mississippi?

The highest county medians for residential land are Lafayette County ($10.80 per sq ft), Madison County ($5.73 per sq ft), Oktibbeha County ($5.67 per sq ft), Tishomingo County ($5.03 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Mississippi?

The lowest county medians are Quitman County ($0.44 per sq ft), Jefferson County ($0.52 per sq ft), Claiborne County ($0.54 per sq ft), Holmes County ($0.58 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Mississippi?

The typical (median) home in Mississippi is worth about $170,000, with entry-level homes around $98,800 and upper-end homes around $259,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Mississippi?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,030 a month in Mississippi, a 1-bedroom about $875 and a 3-bedroom about $1,320 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Mississippi?

Farmland averages about $3,650 an acre in Mississippi (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
