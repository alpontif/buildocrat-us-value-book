Land and home values in Barrow County, Georgia

Barrow County (suburban, Atlanta-Sandy Springs-Roswell, GA) covers 161 square miles and 18 census tracts. Residential land is about $4.27 per sq ft at the county median, from $3.63 in its most rural tracts to $6.75 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $323,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,540 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Barrow County, GA?

Residential land in Barrow County, GA is about $4.27 per square foot, or $186,001 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $46,500. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Barrow County, GA?

The typical (median) home in Barrow County, GA is worth about $323,000, with entry-level homes around $238,000 and upper-end homes around $393,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Barrow County, GA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,540 a month in Barrow County, GA, a 1-bedroom about $1,400 and a 3-bedroom about $1,840 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Barrow County, GA?

Farmland averages about $12,700 an acre in Barrow County, GA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
