Land and home values in Toombs County, Georgia

Toombs County (small town, Vidalia, GA) covers 364 square miles and 9 census tracts. Residential land is about $2.29 per sq ft at the county median, from $0.93 in its most rural tracts to $3.69 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $153,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,040 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Toombs County, GA?

Residential land in Toombs County, GA is about $2.29 per square foot, or $99,927 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $24,982. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Toombs County, GA?

The typical (median) home in Toombs County, GA is worth about $153,000, with entry-level homes around $92,600 and upper-end homes around $216,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Toombs County, GA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,040 a month in Toombs County, GA, a 1-bedroom about $835 and a 3-bedroom about $1,380 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Toombs County, GA?

Farmland averages about $4,270 an acre in Toombs County, GA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
