Land and home values in Lowndes County, Georgia

Lowndes County (metro core, Valdosta, GA) covers 497 square miles and 29 census tracts. Residential land is about $3.35 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.28 in its most rural tracts to $5.21 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $232,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,290 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Lowndes County, GA?

Residential land in Lowndes County, GA is about $3.35 per square foot, or $145,970 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $36,492. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Lowndes County, GA?

The typical (median) home in Lowndes County, GA is worth about $232,000, with entry-level homes around $151,000 and upper-end homes around $296,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Lowndes County, GA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,290 a month in Lowndes County, GA, a 1-bedroom about $990 and a 3-bedroom about $1,720 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Lowndes County, GA?

Farmland averages about $5,000 an acre in Lowndes County, GA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
