Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Georgia?

Residential land in Georgia is about $4.92 per square foot, or $214,185 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $53,546. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Georgia?

The highest county medians for residential land are Greene County ($21.20 per sq ft), Putnam County ($20.60 per sq ft), Lincoln County ($20.10 per sq ft), Hart County ($16.10 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Georgia?

The lowest county medians are Stewart County ($0.20 per sq ft), Webster County ($0.28 per sq ft), Warren County ($0.31 per sq ft), Taliaferro County ($0.33 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Georgia?

The typical (median) home in Georgia is worth about $301,000, with entry-level homes around $220,000 and upper-end homes around $411,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Georgia?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,580 a month in Georgia, a 1-bedroom about $1,420 and a 3-bedroom about $1,950 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Georgia?

Farmland averages about $4,950 an acre in Georgia (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
