Land and home values in Jefferson County, Alabama

Jefferson County (metro core, Birmingham, AL) covers 1,112 square miles and 189 census tracts. Residential land is about $3.62 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.31 in its most rural tracts to $28.00 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $231,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,300 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Jefferson County, AL?

Residential land in Jefferson County, AL is about $3.62 per square foot, or $157,557 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $39,389. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Jefferson County, AL?

The typical (median) home in Jefferson County, AL is worth about $231,000, with entry-level homes around $172,000 and upper-end homes around $323,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Jefferson County, AL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,300 a month in Jefferson County, AL, a 1-bedroom about $1,180 and a 3-bedroom about $1,620 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Jefferson County, AL?

Farmland averages about $8,380 an acre in Jefferson County, AL (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
