Land and home values in Marshall County, Alabama

Marshall County (small town, Albertville, AL) covers 566 square miles and 26 census tracts. Residential land is about $2.81 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.85 in its most rural tracts to $6.71 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $216,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $930 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Marshall County, AL?

Residential land in Marshall County, AL is about $2.81 per square foot, or $122,578 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $30,644. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Marshall County, AL?

The typical (median) home in Marshall County, AL is worth about $216,000, with entry-level homes around $130,000 and upper-end homes around $331,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Marshall County, AL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $930 a month in Marshall County, AL, a 1-bedroom about $745 and a 3-bedroom about $1,200 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Marshall County, AL?

Farmland averages about $6,940 an acre in Marshall County, AL (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
