Land and home values in Sumter County, Alabama

Sumter County (rural) covers 904 square miles and 5 census tracts. Residential land is about $0.62 per sq ft at the county median, from $0.43 in its most rural tracts to $1.02 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $95,900 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $835 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Sumter County, AL?

Residential land in Sumter County, AL is about $0.62 per square foot, or $27,007 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $6,752. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Sumter County, AL?

The typical (median) home in Sumter County, AL is worth about $95,900, with entry-level homes around $16,400 and upper-end homes around $240,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Sumter County, AL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $835 a month in Sumter County, AL, a 1-bedroom about $650 and a 3-bedroom about $1,080 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Sumter County, AL?

Farmland averages about $2,880 an acre in Sumter County, AL (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
