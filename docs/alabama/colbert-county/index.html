Land and home values in Colbert County, Alabama

Colbert County (metro core, Florence-Muscle Shoals, AL) covers 593 square miles and 15 census tracts. Residential land is about $3.55 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.12 in its most rural tracts to $5.47 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $190,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $980 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Colbert County, AL?

Residential land in Colbert County, AL is about $3.55 per square foot, or $154,464 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $38,616. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Colbert County, AL?

The typical (median) home in Colbert County, AL is worth about $190,000, with entry-level homes around $120,000 and upper-end homes around $279,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Colbert County, AL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $980 a month in Colbert County, AL, a 1-bedroom about $760 and a 3-bedroom about $1,220 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Colbert County, AL?

Farmland averages about $4,260 an acre in Colbert County, AL (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
