Land and home values in Alabama

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Alabama?

Residential land in Alabama is about $3.24 per square foot, or $141,222 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $35,305. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Alabama?

The highest county medians for residential land are Tallapoosa County ($30.40 per sq ft), Winston County ($14.50 per sq ft), Baldwin County ($7.32 per sq ft), Coosa County ($7.11 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Alabama?

The lowest county medians are Greene County ($0.48 per sq ft), Wilcox County ($0.54 per sq ft), Bullock County ($0.58 per sq ft), Lowndes County ($0.60 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Alabama?

The typical (median) home in Alabama is worth about $213,000, with entry-level homes around $131,000 and upper-end homes around $312,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Alabama?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,080 a month in Alabama, a 1-bedroom about $915 and a 3-bedroom about $1,380 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Alabama?

Farmland averages about $4,250 an acre in Alabama (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
