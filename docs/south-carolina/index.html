Land and home values in South Carolina

Across South Carolina's 46 counties, residential land is about $5.41 per sq ft at the state median ($235,660 an acre). The typical home is worth about $277,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,340 a month. Largest city: Charleston; capital: Columbia. Values as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in South Carolina?

Residential land in South Carolina is about $5.41 per square foot, or $235,660 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $58,915. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in South Carolina?

The highest county medians for residential land are Charleston County ($19.90 per sq ft), Georgetown County ($13.30 per sq ft), Beaufort County ($11.80 per sq ft), Dorchester County ($7.91 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in South Carolina?

The lowest county medians are Allendale County ($0.63 per sq ft), Hampton County ($0.79 per sq ft), Bamberg County ($0.83 per sq ft), Marlboro County ($1.14 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in South Carolina?

The typical (median) home in South Carolina is worth about $277,000, with entry-level homes around $191,000 and upper-end homes around $393,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in South Carolina?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,340 a month in South Carolina, a 1-bedroom about $1,180 and a 3-bedroom about $1,660 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in South Carolina?

Farmland averages about $4,900 an acre in South Carolina (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
