Land and home values in York County, South Carolina

York County (suburban, Charlotte-Concord-Gastonia, NC-SC) covers 681 square miles and 55 census tracts. Residential land is about $7.49 per sq ft at the county median, from $3.79 in its most rural tracts to $14.60 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $386,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,500 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in York County, SC?

Residential land in York County, SC is about $7.49 per square foot, or $326,308 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $81,577. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in York County, SC?

The typical (median) home in York County, SC is worth about $386,000, with entry-level homes around $279,000 and upper-end homes around $480,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in York County, SC?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,500 a month in York County, SC, a 1-bedroom about $1,370 and a 3-bedroom about $1,840 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in York County, SC?

Farmland averages about $10,400 an acre in York County, SC (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
