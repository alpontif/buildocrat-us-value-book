Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Florida?

Residential land in Florida is about $11.40 per square foot, or $496,584 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $124,146. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Florida?

The highest county medians for residential land are Monroe County ($55.80 per sq ft), Walton County ($39.20 per sq ft), Miami-Dade County ($31.10 per sq ft), Collier County ($26.90 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Florida?

The lowest county medians are Lafayette County ($0.81 per sq ft), Hamilton County ($0.84 per sq ft), Washington County ($0.90 per sq ft), Jefferson County ($1.48 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Florida?

The typical (median) home in Florida is worth about $365,000, with entry-level homes around $264,000 and upper-end homes around $481,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Florida?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,040 a month in Florida, a 1-bedroom about $1,740 and a 3-bedroom about $2,640 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Florida?

Farmland averages about $9,150 an acre in Florida (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
