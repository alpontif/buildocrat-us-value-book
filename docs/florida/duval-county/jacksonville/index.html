Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Jacksonville, FL?

Residential land in Jacksonville, FL is about $4.12 per square foot, or $179,337 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $44,834. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Jacksonville, FL?

The typical (median) home in Jacksonville, FL is worth about $306,000, with entry-level homes around $99,600 and upper-end homes around $306,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Jacksonville, FL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,070 a month in Jacksonville, FL, a 1-bedroom about $893 and a 3-bedroom about $1,320 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
