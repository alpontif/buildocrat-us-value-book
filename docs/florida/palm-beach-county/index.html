Land and home values in Palm Beach County, Florida

Palm Beach County (metro core, Miami-Fort Lauderdale-West Palm Beach, FL) covers 1,964 square miles and 373 census tracts. Residential land is about $19.40 per sq ft at the county median, from $6.86 in its most rural tracts to $113 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $464,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,450 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Palm Beach County, FL?

Residential land in Palm Beach County, FL is about $19.40 per square foot, or $845,064 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $211,266. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Palm Beach County, FL?

The typical (median) home in Palm Beach County, FL is worth about $464,000, with entry-level homes around $344,000 and upper-end homes around $649,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Palm Beach County, FL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,450 a month in Palm Beach County, FL, a 1-bedroom about $2,070 and a 3-bedroom about $3,180 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Palm Beach County, FL?

Farmland averages about $10,200 an acre in Palm Beach County, FL (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
