Land and home values in Hillsborough County, Florida

Hillsborough County (metro core, Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater, FL) covers 1,023 square miles and 335 census tracts. Residential land is about $11.00 per sq ft at the county median, from $5.55 in its most rural tracts to $39.20 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $373,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,200 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Hillsborough County, FL?

Residential land in Hillsborough County, FL is about $11.00 per square foot, or $479,160 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $119,790. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Hillsborough County, FL?

The typical (median) home in Hillsborough County, FL is worth about $373,000, with entry-level homes around $275,000 and upper-end homes around $487,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Hillsborough County, FL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,200 a month in Hillsborough County, FL, a 1-bedroom about $1,890 and a 3-bedroom about $2,820 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Hillsborough County, FL?

Farmland averages about $20,200 an acre in Hillsborough County, FL (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
