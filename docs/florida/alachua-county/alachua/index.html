Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Alachua, FL?

Residential land in Alachua, FL is about $6.75 per square foot, or $294,030 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $73,508. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Alachua, FL?

The typical (median) home in Alachua, FL is worth about $321,000, with entry-level homes around $259,000 and upper-end homes around $445,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Alachua, FL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,400 a month in Alachua, FL, a 1-bedroom about $1,160 and a 3-bedroom about $1,770 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
