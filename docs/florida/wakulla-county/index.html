Land and home values in Wakulla County, Florida

Wakulla County (suburban, Tallahassee, FL) covers 606 square miles and 9 census tracts. Residential land is about $3.56 per sq ft at the county median, from $2.23 in its most rural tracts to $4.53 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $255,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,240 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Wakulla County, FL?

Residential land in Wakulla County, FL is about $3.56 per square foot, or $155,117 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $38,779. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Wakulla County, FL?

The typical (median) home in Wakulla County, FL is worth about $255,000, with entry-level homes around $165,000 and upper-end homes around $385,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Wakulla County, FL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,240 a month in Wakulla County, FL, a 1-bedroom about $1,120 and a 3-bedroom about $1,590 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Wakulla County, FL?

Farmland averages about $12,100 an acre in Wakulla County, FL (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
