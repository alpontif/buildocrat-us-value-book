Land and home values in Franklin County, Vermont

Franklin County (suburban, Burlington-South Burlington, VT) covers 631 square miles and 11 census tracts. Residential land is about $8.94 per sq ft at the county median, from $4.31 in its most rural tracts to $11.20 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $353,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,860 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Franklin County, VT?

Residential land in Franklin County, VT is about $8.94 per square foot, or $389,601 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $97,400. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Franklin County, VT?

The typical (median) home in Franklin County, VT is worth about $353,000, with entry-level homes around $264,000 and upper-end homes around $471,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Franklin County, VT?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,860 a month in Franklin County, VT, a 1-bedroom about $1,440 and a 3-bedroom about $2,380 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Franklin County, VT?

Farmland averages about $4,540 an acre in Franklin County, VT (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
