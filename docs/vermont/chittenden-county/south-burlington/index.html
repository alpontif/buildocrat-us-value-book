Questions people ask

How much is land worth in South Burlington, VT?

Residential land in South Burlington, VT is about $23.70 per square foot, or $1.03M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $258,093. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in South Burlington, VT?

The typical (median) home in South Burlington, VT is worth about $516,000, with entry-level homes around $516,000 and upper-end homes around $841,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in South Burlington, VT?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,760 a month in South Burlington, VT, a 1-bedroom about $2,140 and a 3-bedroom about $3,540 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
