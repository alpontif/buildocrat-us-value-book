Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Wallingford Center, CT?

Residential land in Wallingford Center, CT is about $13.90 per square foot, or $605,484 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $151,371. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Wallingford Center, CT?

The typical (median) home in Wallingford Center, CT is worth about $379,000, with entry-level homes around $302,000 and upper-end homes around $442,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Wallingford Center, CT?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,640 a month in Wallingford Center, CT, a 1-bedroom about $1,320 and a 3-bedroom about $2,020 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
