Land and home values in Northwest Hills Planning Region, Connecticut

Northwest Hills Planning Region (small town, Torrington, CT) covers 787 square miles and 37 census tracts. Residential land is about $10.50 per sq ft at the county median, from $4.66 in its most rural tracts to $28.00 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $432,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,670 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Northwest Hills Planning Region, CT?

Residential land in Northwest Hills Planning Region, CT is about $10.50 per square foot, or $457,380 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $114,345. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Northwest Hills Planning Region, CT?

The typical (median) home in Northwest Hills Planning Region, CT is worth about $432,000, with entry-level homes around $295,000 and upper-end homes around $560,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Northwest Hills Planning Region, CT?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,670 a month in Northwest Hills Planning Region, CT, a 1-bedroom about $1,350 and a 3-bedroom about $2,150 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Northwest Hills Planning Region, CT?

Farmland averages about $14,600 an acre in Northwest Hills Planning Region, CT (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
