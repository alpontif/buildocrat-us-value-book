Land and home values in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, Connecticut

Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region (small town, Putnam, CT) covers 554 square miles and 26 census tracts. Residential land is about $7.83 per sq ft at the county median, from $6.36 in its most rural tracts to $10.80 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $372,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,650 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, CT?

Residential land in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, CT is about $7.83 per square foot, or $340,857 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $85,214. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, CT?

The typical (median) home in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, CT is worth about $372,000, with entry-level homes around $282,000 and upper-end homes around $474,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, CT?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,650 a month in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, CT, a 1-bedroom about $1,350 and a 3-bedroom about $2,120 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, CT?

Farmland averages about $14,600 an acre in Northeastern Connecticut Planning Region, CT (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
