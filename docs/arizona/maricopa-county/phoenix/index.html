Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Phoenix, AZ?

Residential land in Phoenix, AZ is about $13.10 per square foot, or $570,636 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $142,659. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Phoenix, AZ?

The typical (median) home in Phoenix, AZ is worth about $442,000, with entry-level homes around $191,000 and upper-end homes around $442,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Phoenix, AZ?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,480 a month in Phoenix, AZ, a 1-bedroom about $1,270 and a 3-bedroom about $1,970 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
