Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Arizona?

Residential land in Arizona is about $10.90 per square foot, or $474,804 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $118,701. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Arizona?

The highest county medians for residential land are Coconino County ($19.60 per sq ft), Maricopa County ($14.00 per sq ft), La Paz County ($12.70 per sq ft), Pima County ($9.13 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Arizona?

The lowest county medians are Greenlee County ($1.69 per sq ft), Cochise County ($4.04 per sq ft), Graham County ($4.38 per sq ft), Apache County ($4.58 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Arizona?

The typical (median) home in Arizona is worth about $400,000, with entry-level homes around $314,000 and upper-end homes around $496,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Arizona?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,860 a month in Arizona, a 1-bedroom about $1,560 and a 3-bedroom about $2,500 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Arizona?

Farmland averages about $4,300 an acre in Arizona (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
