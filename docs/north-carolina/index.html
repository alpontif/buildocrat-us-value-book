Questions people ask

How much is land worth in North Carolina?

Residential land in North Carolina is about $5.94 per square foot, or $258,921 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $64,730. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in North Carolina?

The highest county medians for residential land are Dare County ($22.60 per sq ft), Graham County ($22.60 per sq ft), Montgomery County ($17.90 per sq ft), Orange County ($16.70 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in North Carolina?

The lowest county medians are Washington County ($1.21 per sq ft), Hyde County ($1.33 per sq ft), Anson County ($1.49 per sq ft), Richmond County ($1.53 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in North Carolina?

The typical (median) home in North Carolina is worth about $296,000, with entry-level homes around $203,000 and upper-end homes around $413,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in North Carolina?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,380 a month in North Carolina, a 1-bedroom about $1,220 and a 3-bedroom about $1,760 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in North Carolina?

Farmland averages about $5,750 an acre in North Carolina (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
