Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Hawaii?

Residential land in Hawaii is about $67.00 per square foot, or $2.92M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $729,630. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Hawaii?

The highest county medians for residential land are Honolulu County ($83.90 per sq ft), Kalawao County ($70.90 per sq ft), Maui County ($52.70 per sq ft), Kauai County ($42.30 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Hawaii?

The lowest county medians are Hawaii County ($24.40 per sq ft), Kauai County ($42.30 per sq ft), Maui County ($52.70 per sq ft), Kalawao County ($70.90 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Hawaii?

The typical (median) home in Hawaii is worth about $929,000, with entry-level homes around $652,000 and upper-end homes around $1.18M, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Hawaii?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,680 a month in Hawaii, a 1-bedroom about $2,060 and a 3-bedroom about $3,660 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
