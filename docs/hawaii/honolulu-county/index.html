Land and home values in Honolulu County, Hawaii

Honolulu County (metro core, Urban Honolulu, HI) covers 601 square miles and 330 census tracts. Residential land is about $83.90 per sq ft at the county median, from $39.00 in its most rural tracts to $154 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $940,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,800 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Honolulu County, HI?

Residential land in Honolulu County, HI is about $83.90 per square foot, or $3.65M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $913,671. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Honolulu County, HI?

The typical (median) home in Honolulu County, HI is worth about $940,000, with entry-level homes around $674,000 and upper-end homes around $1.17M, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Honolulu County, HI?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,800 a month in Honolulu County, HI, a 1-bedroom about $2,140 and a 3-bedroom about $3,900 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Honolulu County, HI?

Farmland averages about $29,900 an acre in Honolulu County, HI (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
