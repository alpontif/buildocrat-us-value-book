Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Kahului, HI?

Residential land in Kahului, HI is about $47.20 per square foot, or $2.06M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $514,008. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Kahului, HI?

The typical (median) home in Kahului, HI is worth about $968,000, with entry-level homes around $854,000 and upper-end homes around $1.14M, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Kahului, HI?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,970 a month in Kahului, HI, a 1-bedroom about $2,310 and a 3-bedroom about $3,930 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
