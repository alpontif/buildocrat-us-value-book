Land and home values in Louisiana

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Louisiana?

Residential land in Louisiana is about $3.64 per square foot, or $158,384 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $39,596. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Louisiana?

The highest county medians for residential land are Orleans Parish ($15.00 per sq ft), Jefferson Parish ($10.30 per sq ft), Pointe Coupee Parish ($7.41 per sq ft), West Feliciana Parish ($7.32 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Louisiana?

The lowest county medians are Claiborne Parish ($0.79 per sq ft), Madison Parish ($0.92 per sq ft), Tensas Parish ($0.96 per sq ft), Richland Parish ($0.97 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Louisiana?

The typical (median) home in Louisiana is worth about $212,000, with entry-level homes around $138,000 and upper-end homes around $299,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Louisiana?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,180 a month in Louisiana, a 1-bedroom about $1,000 and a 3-bedroom about $1,500 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Louisiana?

Farmland averages about $3,950 an acre in Louisiana (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
