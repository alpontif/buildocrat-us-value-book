Land and home values in Richland Parish, Louisiana

Richland Parish (suburban, Monroe, LA) covers 556 square miles and 6 census tracts. Residential land is about $0.97 per sq ft at the county median, from $0.84 in its most rural tracts to $2.28 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $184,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $930 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Richland Parish, LA?

Residential land in Richland Parish, LA is about $0.97 per square foot, or $42,384 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $10,596. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Richland Parish, LA?

The typical (median) home in Richland Parish, LA is worth about $184,000, with entry-level homes around $96,600 and upper-end homes around $328,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Richland Parish, LA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $930 a month in Richland Parish, LA, a 1-bedroom about $745 and a 3-bedroom about $1,140 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Richland Parish, LA?

Farmland averages about $3,850 an acre in Richland Parish, LA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
