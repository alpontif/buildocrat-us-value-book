Land and home values in Tangipahoa Parish, Louisiana

Tangipahoa Parish (metro core, Hammond, LA) covers 791 square miles and 31 census tracts. Residential land is about $2.77 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.48 in its most rural tracts to $4.99 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $217,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,120 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Tangipahoa Parish, LA?

Residential land in Tangipahoa Parish, LA is about $2.77 per square foot, or $120,879 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $30,220. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Tangipahoa Parish, LA?

The typical (median) home in Tangipahoa Parish, LA is worth about $217,000, with entry-level homes around $135,000 and upper-end homes around $313,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Tangipahoa Parish, LA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,120 a month in Tangipahoa Parish, LA, a 1-bedroom about $970 and a 3-bedroom about $1,400 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Tangipahoa Parish, LA?

Farmland averages about $7,970 an acre in Tangipahoa Parish, LA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
