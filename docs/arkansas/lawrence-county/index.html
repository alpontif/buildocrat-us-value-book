Land and home values in Lawrence County, Arkansas

Lawrence County (rural) covers 588 square miles and 6 census tracts. Residential land is about $1.47 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.04 in its most rural tracts to $2.04 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $115,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $980 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Lawrence County, AR?

Residential land in Lawrence County, AR is about $1.47 per square foot, or $64,120 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $16,030. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Lawrence County, AR?

The typical (median) home in Lawrence County, AR is worth about $115,000, with entry-level homes around $64,900 and upper-end homes around $183,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Lawrence County, AR?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $980 a month in Lawrence County, AR, a 1-bedroom about $755 and a 3-bedroom about $1,240 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Lawrence County, AR?

Farmland averages about $5,280 an acre in Lawrence County, AR (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
