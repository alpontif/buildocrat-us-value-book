Land and home values in Grant County, Arkansas

Grant County (suburban, Little Rock-North Little Rock-Conway, AR) covers 632 square miles and 5 census tracts. Residential land is about $2.73 per sq ft at the county median, from $2.04 in its most rural tracts to $3.75 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $130,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $985 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Grant County, AR?

Residential land in Grant County, AR is about $2.73 per square foot, or $118,701 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $29,675. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Grant County, AR?

The typical (median) home in Grant County, AR is worth about $130,000, with entry-level homes around $88,900 and upper-end homes around $267,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Grant County, AR?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $985 a month in Grant County, AR, a 1-bedroom about $815 and a 3-bedroom about $1,250 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Grant County, AR?

Farmland averages about $4,480 an acre in Grant County, AR (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
