Land and home values in Arkansas

Across Arkansas's 75 counties, residential land is about $2.70 per sq ft at the state median ($117,786 an acre). The typical home is worth about $191,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,030 a month. Largest city: Little Rock; capital: Little Rock. Values as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Arkansas?

Residential land in Arkansas is about $2.70 per square foot, or $117,786 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $29,447. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Arkansas?

The highest county medians for residential land are Cleburne County ($7.11 per sq ft), Washington County ($6.07 per sq ft), Benton County ($4.87 per sq ft), Pulaski County ($4.11 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Arkansas?

The lowest county medians are Lee County ($0.51 per sq ft), Phillips County ($0.57 per sq ft), Nevada County ($0.63 per sq ft), Prairie County ($0.67 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Arkansas?

The typical (median) home in Arkansas is worth about $191,000, with entry-level homes around $120,000 and upper-end homes around $281,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Arkansas?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,030 a month in Arkansas, a 1-bedroom about $840 and a 3-bedroom about $1,380 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Arkansas?

Farmland averages about $4,350 an acre in Arkansas (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
