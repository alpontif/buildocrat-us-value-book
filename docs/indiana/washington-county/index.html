Land and home values in Washington County, Indiana

Washington County (suburban, Louisville/Jefferson County, KY-IN) covers 514 square miles and 7 census tracts. Residential land is about $2.61 per sq ft at the county median, from $2.47 in its most rural tracts to $3.01 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $204,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $990 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Washington County, IN?

Residential land in Washington County, IN is about $2.61 per square foot, or $113,517 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $28,379. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Washington County, IN?

The typical (median) home in Washington County, IN is worth about $204,000, with entry-level homes around $114,000 and upper-end homes around $289,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Washington County, IN?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $990 a month in Washington County, IN, a 1-bedroom about $750 and a 3-bedroom about $1,180 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Washington County, IN?

Farmland averages about $7,110 an acre in Washington County, IN (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
