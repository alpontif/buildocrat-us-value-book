Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Indiana?

Residential land in Indiana is about $3.91 per square foot, or $170,363 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $42,591. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Indiana?

The highest county medians for residential land are Steuben County ($15.80 per sq ft), Brown County ($9.66 per sq ft), Hamilton County ($9.21 per sq ft), LaGrange County ($8.69 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Indiana?

The lowest county medians are Crawford County ($0.85 per sq ft), Martin County ($1.34 per sq ft), Vermillion County ($1.36 per sq ft), Knox County ($1.41 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Indiana?

The typical (median) home in Indiana is worth about $234,000, with entry-level homes around $167,000 and upper-end homes around $326,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Indiana?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,220 a month in Indiana, a 1-bedroom about $1,020 and a 3-bedroom about $1,540 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Indiana?

Farmland averages about $9,150 an acre in Indiana (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
