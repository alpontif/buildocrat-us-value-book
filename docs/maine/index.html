Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Maine?

Residential land in Maine is about $10.90 per square foot, or $474,804 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $118,701. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Maine?

The highest county medians for residential land are Cumberland County ($18.10 per sq ft), Lincoln County ($16.40 per sq ft), York County ($15.90 per sq ft), Knox County ($15.50 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Maine?

The lowest county medians are Aroostook County ($2.63 per sq ft), Washington County ($4.13 per sq ft), Penobscot County ($4.70 per sq ft), Somerset County ($4.71 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Maine?

The typical (median) home in Maine is worth about $323,000, with entry-level homes around $221,000 and upper-end homes around $452,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Maine?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,640 a month in Maine, a 1-bedroom about $1,300 and a 3-bedroom about $2,120 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Maine?

Farmland averages about $3,400 an acre in Maine (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
