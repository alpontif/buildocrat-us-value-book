Land and home values in Penobscot County, Maine

Penobscot County (metro core, Bangor, ME) covers 3,397 square miles and 46 census tracts. Residential land is about $4.70 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.84 in its most rural tracts to $6.32 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $226,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,650 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Penobscot County, ME?

Residential land in Penobscot County, ME is about $4.70 per square foot, or $204,906 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $51,227. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Penobscot County, ME?

The typical (median) home in Penobscot County, ME is worth about $226,000, with entry-level homes around $131,000 and upper-end homes around $327,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Penobscot County, ME?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,650 a month in Penobscot County, ME, a 1-bedroom about $1,300 and a 3-bedroom about $2,140 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Penobscot County, ME?

Farmland averages about $4,220 an acre in Penobscot County, ME (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
