Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Maryland?

Residential land in Maryland is about $15.10 per square foot, or $657,756 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $164,439. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Maryland?

The highest county medians for residential land are Worcester County ($44.30 per sq ft), Montgomery County ($29.00 per sq ft), Howard County ($24.90 per sq ft), Anne Arundel County ($22.20 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Maryland?

The lowest county medians are Allegany County ($2.77 per sq ft), Somerset County ($3.87 per sq ft), Wicomico County ($4.78 per sq ft), Garrett County ($4.82 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Maryland?

The typical (median) home in Maryland is worth about $435,000, with entry-level homes around $340,000 and upper-end homes around $545,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Maryland?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,010 a month in Maryland, a 1-bedroom about $1,720 and a 3-bedroom about $2,550 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Maryland?

Farmland averages about $9,950 an acre in Maryland (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
