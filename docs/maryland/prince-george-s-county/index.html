Land and home values in Prince George's County, Maryland

Prince George's County (metro core, Washington-Arlington-Alexandria, DC-VA-MD-WV) covers 483 square miles and 214 census tracts. Residential land is about $15.10 per sq ft at the county median, from $10.70 in its most rural tracts to $20.00 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $440,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,160 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Prince George's County, MD?

Residential land in Prince George's County, MD is about $15.10 per square foot, or $657,756 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $164,439. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Prince George's County, MD?

The typical (median) home in Prince George's County, MD is worth about $440,000, with entry-level homes around $359,000 and upper-end homes around $515,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Prince George's County, MD?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,160 a month in Prince George's County, MD, a 1-bedroom about $1,930 and a 3-bedroom about $2,720 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Prince George's County, MD?

Farmland averages about $10,300 an acre in Prince George's County, MD (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
