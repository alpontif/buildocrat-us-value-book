Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Green Valley, MD?

Residential land in Green Valley, MD is about $15.90 per square foot, or $692,604 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $173,151. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Green Valley, MD?

The typical (median) home in Green Valley, MD is worth about $648,000, with entry-level homes around $486,000 and upper-end homes around $824,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Green Valley, MD?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,070 a month in Green Valley, MD, a 1-bedroom about $1,780 and a 3-bedroom about $2,520 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
