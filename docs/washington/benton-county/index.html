Land and home values in Benton County, Washington

Benton County (metro core, Kennewick-Richland, WA) covers 1,700 square miles and 45 census tracts. Residential land is about $8.60 per sq ft at the county median, from $7.28 in its most rural tracts to $12.10 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $394,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,660 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Benton County, WA?

Residential land in Benton County, WA is about $8.60 per square foot, or $374,572 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $93,643. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Benton County, WA?

The typical (median) home in Benton County, WA is worth about $394,000, with entry-level homes around $290,000 and upper-end homes around $504,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Benton County, WA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,660 a month in Benton County, WA, a 1-bedroom about $1,360 and a 3-bedroom about $2,230 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Benton County, WA?

Farmland averages about $4,710 an acre in Benton County, WA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
