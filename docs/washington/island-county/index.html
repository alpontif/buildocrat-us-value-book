Land and home values in Island County, Washington

Island County (small town, Oak Harbor, WA) covers 209 square miles and 24 census tracts. Residential land is about $20.40 per sq ft at the county median, from $13.70 in its most rural tracts to $32.30 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $637,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,690 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Island County, WA?

Residential land in Island County, WA is about $20.40 per square foot, or $888,624 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $222,156. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Island County, WA?

The typical (median) home in Island County, WA is worth about $637,000, with entry-level homes around $453,000 and upper-end homes around $841,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Island County, WA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,690 a month in Island County, WA, a 1-bedroom about $1,370 and a 3-bedroom about $2,260 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Island County, WA?

Farmland averages about $11,400 an acre in Island County, WA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
