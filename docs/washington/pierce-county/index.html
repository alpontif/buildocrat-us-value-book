Land and home values in Pierce County, Washington

Pierce County (metro core, Seattle-Tacoma-Bellevue, WA) covers 1,668 square miles and 193 census tracts. Residential land is about $16.20 per sq ft at the county median, from $11.10 in its most rural tracts to $31.10 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $538,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,090 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Pierce County, WA?

Residential land in Pierce County, WA is about $16.20 per square foot, or $705,672 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $176,418. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Pierce County, WA?

The typical (median) home in Pierce County, WA is worth about $538,000, with entry-level homes around $429,000 and upper-end homes around $704,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Pierce County, WA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,090 a month in Pierce County, WA, a 1-bedroom about $1,700 and a 3-bedroom about $2,900 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Pierce County, WA?

Farmland averages about $11,400 an acre in Pierce County, WA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
