Land and home values in Cumberland County, Pennsylvania

Cumberland County (metro core, Harrisburg-Carlisle, PA) covers 545 square miles and 55 census tracts. Residential land is about $7.75 per sq ft at the county median, from $4.11 in its most rural tracts to $11.00 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $291,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,560 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Cumberland County, PA?

Residential land in Cumberland County, PA is about $7.75 per square foot, or $337,372 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $84,343. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Cumberland County, PA?

The typical (median) home in Cumberland County, PA is worth about $291,000, with entry-level homes around $221,000 and upper-end homes around $405,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Cumberland County, PA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,560 a month in Cumberland County, PA, a 1-bedroom about $1,280 and a 3-bedroom about $2,020 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Cumberland County, PA?

Farmland averages about $11,900 an acre in Cumberland County, PA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
