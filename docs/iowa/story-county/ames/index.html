Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Ames, IA?

Residential land in Ames, IA is about $7.58 per square foot, or $329,967 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $82,492. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Ames, IA?

The typical (median) home in Ames, IA is worth about $305,000, with entry-level homes around $141,000 and upper-end homes around $305,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Ames, IA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,110 a month in Ames, IA, a 1-bedroom about $978 and a 3-bedroom about $1,530 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
