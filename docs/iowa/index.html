Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Iowa?

Residential land in Iowa is about $3.66 per square foot, or $159,386 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $39,847. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Iowa?

The highest county medians for residential land are Dickinson County ($30.50 per sq ft), Johnson County ($7.82 per sq ft), Dallas County ($7.47 per sq ft), Story County ($7.27 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Iowa?

The lowest county medians are Pocahontas County ($0.82 per sq ft), Emmet County ($0.87 per sq ft), Ida County ($0.87 per sq ft), Adams County ($0.93 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Iowa?

The typical (median) home in Iowa is worth about $217,000, with entry-level homes around $151,000 and upper-end homes around $318,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Iowa?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,080 a month in Iowa, a 1-bedroom about $865 and a 3-bedroom about $1,420 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Iowa?

Farmland averages about $10,100 an acre in Iowa (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
