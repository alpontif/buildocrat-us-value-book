Land and home values in Barnstable County, Massachusetts

Barnstable County (metro core, Barnstable Town, MA) covers 394 square miles and 57 census tracts. Residential land is about $35.20 per sq ft at the county median, from $21.10 in its most rural tracts to $73.70 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $727,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,420 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Barnstable County, MA?

Residential land in Barnstable County, MA is about $35.20 per square foot, or $1.53M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $383,328. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Barnstable County, MA?

The typical (median) home in Barnstable County, MA is worth about $727,000, with entry-level homes around $535,000 and upper-end homes around $971,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Barnstable County, MA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,420 a month in Barnstable County, MA, a 1-bedroom about $1,840 and a 3-bedroom about $2,980 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Barnstable County, MA?

Farmland averages about $45,600 an acre in Barnstable County, MA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
