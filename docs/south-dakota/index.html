Land and home values in South Dakota

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in South Dakota?

Residential land in South Dakota is about $4.84 per square foot, or $211,005 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $52,751. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in South Dakota?

The highest county medians for residential land are Deuel County ($15.30 per sq ft), Lawrence County ($10.30 per sq ft), Day County ($8.65 per sq ft), Pennington County ($7.21 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in South Dakota?

The lowest county medians are Oglala Lakota County ($0.28 per sq ft), Corson County ($0.42 per sq ft), Todd County ($0.42 per sq ft), Dewey County ($0.52 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in South Dakota?

The typical (median) home in South Dakota is worth about $266,000, with entry-level homes around $174,000 and upper-end homes around $391,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in South Dakota?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,080 a month in South Dakota, a 1-bedroom about $875 and a 3-bedroom about $1,450 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in South Dakota?

Farmland averages about $3,100 an acre in South Dakota (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
