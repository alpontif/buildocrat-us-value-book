Land and home values in Harrisonburg city, Virginia

Harrisonburg city (metro core, Harrisonburg, VA) covers 17 square miles and 11 census tracts. Residential land is about $7.74 per sq ft at the county median, from $6.62 in its most rural tracts to $11.90 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $313,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,280 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Harrisonburg city, VA?

Residential land in Harrisonburg city, VA is about $7.74 per square foot, or $337,329 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $84,332. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Harrisonburg city, VA?

The typical (median) home in Harrisonburg city, VA is worth about $313,000, with entry-level homes around $255,000 and upper-end homes around $419,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Harrisonburg city, VA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,280 a month in Harrisonburg city, VA, a 1-bedroom about $1,020 and a 3-bedroom about $1,740 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Harrisonburg city, VA?

Farmland averages about $6,300 an acre in Harrisonburg city, VA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
