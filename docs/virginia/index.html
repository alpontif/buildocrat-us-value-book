Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Virginia?

Residential land in Virginia is about $11.20 per square foot, or $487,872 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $121,968. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Virginia?

The highest county medians for residential land are Falls Church city ($75.00 per sq ft), Arlington County ($71.80 per sq ft), Alexandria city ($52.80 per sq ft), Fairfax County ($35.10 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Virginia?

The lowest county medians are Buchanan County ($0.87 per sq ft), Martinsville city ($0.91 per sq ft), Dickenson County ($1.05 per sq ft), Norton city ($1.06 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Virginia?

The typical (median) home in Virginia is worth about $406,000, with entry-level homes around $304,000 and upper-end homes around $526,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Virginia?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,790 a month in Virginia, a 1-bedroom about $1,600 and a 3-bedroom about $2,350 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Virginia?

Farmland averages about $6,300 an acre in Virginia (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
