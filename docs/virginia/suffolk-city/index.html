Land and home values in Suffolk city, Virginia

Suffolk city (suburban, Virginia Beach-Chesapeake-Norfolk, VA-NC) covers 399 square miles and 36 census tracts. Residential land is about $8.51 per sq ft at the county median, from $4.91 in its most rural tracts to $13.20 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $395,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,840 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Suffolk city, VA?

Residential land in Suffolk city, VA is about $8.51 per square foot, or $370,608 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $92,652. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Suffolk city, VA?

The typical (median) home in Suffolk city, VA is worth about $395,000, with entry-level homes around $291,000 and upper-end homes around $484,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Suffolk city, VA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,840 a month in Suffolk city, VA, a 1-bedroom about $1,630 and a 3-bedroom about $2,560 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Suffolk city, VA?

Farmland averages about $6,130 an acre in Suffolk city, VA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
