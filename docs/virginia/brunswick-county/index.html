Land and home values in Brunswick County, Virginia

Brunswick County (rural) covers 566 square miles and 6 census tracts. Residential land is about $18.30 per sq ft at the county median, from $3.56 in its most rural tracts to $29.30 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $134,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,020 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Brunswick County, VA?

Residential land in Brunswick County, VA is about $18.30 per square foot, or $797,148 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $199,287. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Brunswick County, VA?

The typical (median) home in Brunswick County, VA is worth about $134,000, with entry-level homes around $83,900 and upper-end homes around $237,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Brunswick County, VA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,020 a month in Brunswick County, VA, a 1-bedroom about $820 and a 3-bedroom about $1,390 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Brunswick County, VA?

Farmland averages about $3,500 an acre in Brunswick County, VA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
