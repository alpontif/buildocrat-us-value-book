Land and home values in King George County, Virginia

King George County (rural) covers 180 square miles and 6 census tracts. Residential land is about $9.62 per sq ft at the county median, from $9.12 in its most rural tracts to $11.00 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $492,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,400 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in King George County, VA?

Residential land in King George County, VA is about $9.62 per square foot, or $419,221 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $104,805. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in King George County, VA?

The typical (median) home in King George County, VA is worth about $492,000, with entry-level homes around $370,000 and upper-end homes around $659,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in King George County, VA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,400 a month in King George County, VA, a 1-bedroom about $1,150 and a 3-bedroom about $1,950 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in King George County, VA?

Farmland averages about $7,950 an acre in King George County, VA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
