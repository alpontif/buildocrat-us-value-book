Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Lincolnia, VA?

Residential land in Lincolnia, VA is about $31.80 per square foot, or $1.39M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $346,302. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Lincolnia, VA?

The typical (median) home in Lincolnia, VA is worth about $721,000, with entry-level homes around $494,000 and upper-end homes around $769,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Lincolnia, VA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,330 a month in Lincolnia, VA, a 1-bedroom about $2,100 and a 3-bedroom about $2,960 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
