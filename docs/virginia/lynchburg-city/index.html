Land and home values in Lynchburg city, Virginia

Lynchburg city (metro core, Lynchburg, VA) covers 49 square miles and 23 census tracts. Residential land is about $5.05 per sq ft at the county median, from $2.10 in its most rural tracts to $6.60 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $251,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,270 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Lynchburg city, VA?

Residential land in Lynchburg city, VA is about $5.05 per square foot, or $219,978 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $54,994. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Lynchburg city, VA?

The typical (median) home in Lynchburg city, VA is worth about $251,000, with entry-level homes around $190,000 and upper-end homes around $321,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Lynchburg city, VA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,270 a month in Lynchburg city, VA, a 1-bedroom about $1,110 and a 3-bedroom about $1,740 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Lynchburg city, VA?

Farmland averages about $6,300 an acre in Lynchburg city, VA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
