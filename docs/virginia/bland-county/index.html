Land and home values in Bland County, Virginia

Bland County (rural) covers 358 square miles and 2 census tracts. Residential land is about $1.48 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.48 in its most rural tracts to $1.76 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $154,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $905 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Bland County, VA?

Residential land in Bland County, VA is about $1.48 per square foot, or $64,425 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $16,106. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Bland County, VA?

The typical (median) home in Bland County, VA is worth about $154,000, with entry-level homes around $102,000 and upper-end homes around $278,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Bland County, VA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $905 a month in Bland County, VA, a 1-bedroom about $765 and a 3-bedroom about $1,280 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Bland County, VA?

Farmland averages about $3,750 an acre in Bland County, VA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
