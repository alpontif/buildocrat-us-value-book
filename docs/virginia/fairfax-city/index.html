Land and home values in Fairfax city, Virginia

Fairfax city (metro core, Washington-Arlington-Alexandria, DC-VA-MD-WV) covers 6 square miles and 5 census tracts. Residential land is about $35.00 per sq ft at the county median, from $32.50 in its most rural tracts to $38.30 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $856,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,590 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Fairfax city, VA?

Residential land in Fairfax city, VA is about $35.00 per square foot, or $1.52M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $381,150. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Fairfax city, VA?

The typical (median) home in Fairfax city, VA is worth about $856,000, with entry-level homes around $647,000 and upper-end homes around $1.12M, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Fairfax city, VA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,590 a month in Fairfax city, VA, a 1-bedroom about $2,320 and a 3-bedroom about $3,270 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Fairfax city, VA?

Farmland averages about $6,300 an acre in Fairfax city, VA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
