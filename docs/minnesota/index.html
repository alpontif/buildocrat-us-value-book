Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Minnesota?

Residential land in Minnesota is about $8.69 per square foot, or $378,362 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $94,591. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Minnesota?

The highest county medians for residential land are Cass County ($19.70 per sq ft), Becker County ($17.80 per sq ft), Aitkin County ($15.30 per sq ft), Hennepin County ($14.50 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Minnesota?

The lowest county medians are Traverse County ($0.92 per sq ft), Faribault County ($1.20 per sq ft), Kittson County ($1.23 per sq ft), Redwood County ($1.30 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Minnesota?

The typical (median) home in Minnesota is worth about $347,000, with entry-level homes around $258,000 and upper-end homes around $448,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Minnesota?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,540 a month in Minnesota, a 1-bedroom about $1,260 and a 3-bedroom about $2,040 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Minnesota?

Farmland averages about $6,950 an acre in Minnesota (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
