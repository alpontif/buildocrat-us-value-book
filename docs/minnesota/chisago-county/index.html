Land and home values in Chisago County, Minnesota

Chisago County (suburban, Minneapolis-St. Paul-Bloomington, MN-WI) covers 415 square miles and 13 census tracts. Residential land is about $6.26 per sq ft at the county median, from $3.29 in its most rural tracts to $12.30 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $371,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,490 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Chisago County, MN?

Residential land in Chisago County, MN is about $6.26 per square foot, or $272,511 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $68,128. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Chisago County, MN?

The typical (median) home in Chisago County, MN is worth about $371,000, with entry-level homes around $273,000 and upper-end homes around $488,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Chisago County, MN?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,490 a month in Chisago County, MN, a 1-bedroom about $1,220 and a 3-bedroom about $1,980 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Chisago County, MN?

Farmland averages about $8,440 an acre in Chisago County, MN (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
