Land and home values in Rice County, Minnesota

Rice County (small town, Faribault-Northfield, MN) covers 496 square miles and 16 census tracts. Residential land is about $5.78 per sq ft at the county median, from $4.63 in its most rural tracts to $8.81 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $330,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,370 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Rice County, MN?

Residential land in Rice County, MN is about $5.78 per square foot, or $251,864 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $62,966. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Rice County, MN?

The typical (median) home in Rice County, MN is worth about $330,000, with entry-level homes around $255,000 and upper-end homes around $413,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Rice County, MN?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,370 a month in Rice County, MN, a 1-bedroom about $1,040 and a 3-bedroom about $1,770 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Rice County, MN?

Farmland averages about $10,000 an acre in Rice County, MN (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
