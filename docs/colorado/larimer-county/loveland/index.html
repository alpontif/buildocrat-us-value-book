Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Loveland, CO?

Residential land in Loveland, CO is about $13.00 per square foot, or $566,280 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $141,570. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Loveland, CO?

The typical (median) home in Loveland, CO is worth about $491,000, with entry-level homes around $418,000 and upper-end homes around $606,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Loveland, CO?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,570 a month in Loveland, CO, a 1-bedroom about $1,400 and a 3-bedroom about $2,160 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
