Land and home values in Conejos County, Colorado

Conejos County (small town, Alamosa, CO) covers 1,287 square miles and 2 census tracts. Residential land is about $1.57 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.32 in its most rural tracts to $1.57 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $212,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,000 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Conejos County, CO?

Residential land in Conejos County, CO is about $1.57 per square foot, or $68,215 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $17,054. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Conejos County, CO?

The typical (median) home in Conejos County, CO is worth about $212,000, with entry-level homes around $115,000 and upper-end homes around $355,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Conejos County, CO?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,000 a month in Conejos County, CO, a 1-bedroom about $895 and a 3-bedroom about $1,370 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Conejos County, CO?

Farmland averages about $3,690 an acre in Conejos County, CO (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
