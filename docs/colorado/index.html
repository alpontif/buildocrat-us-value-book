Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Colorado?

Residential land in Colorado is about $14.60 per square foot, or $635,976 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $158,994. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Colorado?

The highest county medians for residential land are Pitkin County ($363 per sq ft), San Miguel County ($98.20 per sq ft), Eagle County ($83.40 per sq ft), Routt County ($50.40 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Colorado?

The lowest county medians are Crowley County ($0.69 per sq ft), Baca County ($0.80 per sq ft), Bent County ($0.94 per sq ft), Sedgwick County ($1.00 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Colorado?

The typical (median) home in Colorado is worth about $554,000, with entry-level homes around $432,000 and upper-end homes around $698,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Colorado?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,050 a month in Colorado, a 1-bedroom about $1,720 and a 3-bedroom about $2,710 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Colorado?

Farmland averages about $2,360 an acre in Colorado (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
