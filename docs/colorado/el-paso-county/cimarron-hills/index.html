Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Cimarron Hills, CO?

Residential land in Cimarron Hills, CO is about $7.36 per square foot, or $320,645 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $80,161. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Cimarron Hills, CO?

The typical (median) home in Cimarron Hills, CO is worth about $375,000, with entry-level homes around $243,000 and upper-end homes around $426,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Cimarron Hills, CO?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,640 a month in Cimarron Hills, CO, a 1-bedroom about $1,390 and a 3-bedroom about $2,290 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
