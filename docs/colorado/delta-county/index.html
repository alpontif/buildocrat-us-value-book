Land and home values in Delta County, Colorado

Delta County (rural) covers 1,142 square miles and 9 census tracts. Residential land is about $6.60 per sq ft at the county median, from $4.42 in its most rural tracts to $8.80 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $365,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,180 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Delta County, CO?

Residential land in Delta County, CO is about $6.60 per square foot, or $287,409 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $71,852. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Delta County, CO?

The typical (median) home in Delta County, CO is worth about $365,000, with entry-level homes around $241,000 and upper-end homes around $496,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Delta County, CO?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,180 a month in Delta County, CO, a 1-bedroom about $915 and a 3-bedroom about $1,640 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Delta County, CO?

Farmland averages about $7,080 an acre in Delta County, CO (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
