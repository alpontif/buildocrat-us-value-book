Land and home values in Eagle County, Colorado

Eagle County (small town, Edwards, CO) covers 1,684 square miles and 16 census tracts. Residential land is about $83.40 per sq ft at the county median, from $11.40 in its most rural tracts to $158 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $1.14M and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,400 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Eagle County, CO?

Residential land in Eagle County, CO is about $83.40 per square foot, or $3.63M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $908,226. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Eagle County, CO?

The typical (median) home in Eagle County, CO is worth about $1.14M, with entry-level homes around $654,000 and upper-end homes around $1.77M, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Eagle County, CO?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,400 a month in Eagle County, CO, a 1-bedroom about $1,820 and a 3-bedroom about $2,870 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Eagle County, CO?

Farmland averages about $7,080 an acre in Eagle County, CO (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
