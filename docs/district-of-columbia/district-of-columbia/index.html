Land and home values in District of Columbia, District of Columbia

District of Columbia (metro core, Washington-Arlington-Alexandria, DC-VA-MD-WV) covers 61 square miles and 206 census tracts. Residential land is about $56.20 per sq ft at the county median, from $17.10 in its most rural tracts to $114 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $698,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,420 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in District of Columbia, DC?

Residential land in District of Columbia, DC is about $56.20 per square foot, or $2.45M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $612,018. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in District of Columbia, DC?

The typical (median) home in District of Columbia, DC is worth about $698,000, with entry-level homes around $520,000 and upper-end homes around $925,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in District of Columbia, DC?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,420 a month in District of Columbia, DC, a 1-bedroom about $2,160 and a 3-bedroom about $3,050 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
