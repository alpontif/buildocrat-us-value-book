Questions people ask

How much is land worth in District of Columbia?

Residential land in District of Columbia is about $56.20 per square foot, or $2.45M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $612,018. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land cheapest in District of Columbia?

The lowest county medians are District of Columbia ($56.20 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in District of Columbia?

The typical (median) home in District of Columbia is worth about $698,000, with entry-level homes around $520,000 and upper-end homes around $925,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in District of Columbia?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,420 a month in District of Columbia, a 1-bedroom about $2,160 and a 3-bedroom about $3,050 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
