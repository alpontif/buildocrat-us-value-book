Land and home values in Charlevoix County, Michigan

Charlevoix County (rural) covers 416 square miles and 14 census tracts. Residential land is about $15.80 per sq ft at the county median, from $9.18 in its most rural tracts to $45.90 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $354,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,130 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Charlevoix County, MI?

Residential land in Charlevoix County, MI is about $15.80 per square foot, or $688,248 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $172,062. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Charlevoix County, MI?

The typical (median) home in Charlevoix County, MI is worth about $354,000, with entry-level homes around $213,000 and upper-end homes around $521,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Charlevoix County, MI?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,130 a month in Charlevoix County, MI, a 1-bedroom about $875 and a 3-bedroom about $1,570 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Charlevoix County, MI?

Farmland averages about $6,280 an acre in Charlevoix County, MI (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
