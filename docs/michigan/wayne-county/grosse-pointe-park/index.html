Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Grosse Pointe Park, MI?

Residential land in Grosse Pointe Park, MI is about $10.30 per square foot, or $448,668 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $112,167. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Grosse Pointe Park, MI?

The typical (median) home in Grosse Pointe Park, MI is worth about $548,000, with entry-level homes around $231,000 and upper-end homes around $548,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Grosse Pointe Park, MI?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,550 a month in Grosse Pointe Park, MI, a 1-bedroom about $1,250 and a 3-bedroom about $1,920 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
