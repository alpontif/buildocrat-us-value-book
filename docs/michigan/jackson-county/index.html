Land and home values in Jackson County, Michigan

Jackson County (metro core, Jackson, MI) covers 702 square miles and 46 census tracts. Residential land is about $4.20 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.52 in its most rural tracts to $11.10 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $239,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,220 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Jackson County, MI?

Residential land in Jackson County, MI is about $4.20 per square foot, or $182,952 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $45,738. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Jackson County, MI?

The typical (median) home in Jackson County, MI is worth about $239,000, with entry-level homes around $137,000 and upper-end homes around $309,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Jackson County, MI?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,220 a month in Jackson County, MI, a 1-bedroom about $995 and a 3-bedroom about $1,620 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Jackson County, MI?

Farmland averages about $6,340 an acre in Jackson County, MI (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
