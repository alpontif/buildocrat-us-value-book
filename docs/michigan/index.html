Land and home values in Michigan

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Michigan?

Residential land in Michigan is about $5.50 per square foot, or $239,624 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $59,906. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Michigan?

The highest county medians for residential land are Antrim County ($35.70 per sq ft), Leelanau County ($24.90 per sq ft), Cheboygan County ($23.90 per sq ft), Emmet County ($21.30 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Michigan?

The lowest county medians are Gogebic County ($1.26 per sq ft), Ontonagon County ($1.36 per sq ft), Alpena County ($1.77 per sq ft), Menominee County ($1.85 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Michigan?

The typical (median) home in Michigan is worth about $252,000, with entry-level homes around $179,000 and upper-end homes around $346,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Michigan?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,300 a month in Michigan, a 1-bedroom about $1,040 and a 3-bedroom about $1,620 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Michigan?

Farmland averages about $7,000 an acre in Michigan (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
