Land and home values in Muskegon County, Michigan

Muskegon County (metro core, Muskegon-Norton Shores, MI) covers 504 square miles and 46 census tracts. Residential land is about $4.43 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.41 in its most rural tracts to $12.50 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $234,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,280 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Muskegon County, MI?

Residential land in Muskegon County, MI is about $4.43 per square foot, or $193,101 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $48,275. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Muskegon County, MI?

The typical (median) home in Muskegon County, MI is worth about $234,000, with entry-level homes around $154,000 and upper-end homes around $332,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Muskegon County, MI?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,280 a month in Muskegon County, MI, a 1-bedroom about $980 and a 3-bedroom about $1,550 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Muskegon County, MI?

Farmland averages about $7,090 an acre in Muskegon County, MI (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
