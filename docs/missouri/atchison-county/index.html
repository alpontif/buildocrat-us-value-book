Land and home values in Atchison County, Missouri

Atchison County (rural) covers 547 square miles and 2 census tracts. Residential land is about $0.54 per sq ft at the county median, from $0.54 in its most rural tracts to $0.72 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $103,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $875 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Atchison County, MO?

Residential land in Atchison County, MO is about $0.54 per square foot, or $23,610 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $5,902. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Atchison County, MO?

The typical (median) home in Atchison County, MO is worth about $103,000, with entry-level homes around $58,600 and upper-end homes around $178,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Atchison County, MO?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $875 a month in Atchison County, MO, a 1-bedroom about $665 and a 3-bedroom about $1,050 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Atchison County, MO?

Farmland averages about $7,650 an acre in Atchison County, MO (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
