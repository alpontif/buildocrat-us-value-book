Land and home values in Missouri

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Missouri?

Residential land in Missouri is about $3.68 per square foot, or $160,475 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $40,119. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Missouri?

The highest county medians for residential land are Camden County ($16.60 per sq ft), Putnam County ($12.40 per sq ft), St. Louis County ($8.16 per sq ft), Morgan County ($7.94 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Missouri?

The lowest county medians are Knox County ($0.52 per sq ft), Atchison County ($0.54 per sq ft), Shelby County ($0.55 per sq ft), Worth County ($0.55 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Missouri?

The typical (median) home in Missouri is worth about $243,000, with entry-level homes around $169,000 and upper-end homes around $343,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Missouri?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,130 a month in Missouri, a 1-bedroom about $935 and a 3-bedroom about $1,480 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Missouri?

Farmland averages about $5,200 an acre in Missouri (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
