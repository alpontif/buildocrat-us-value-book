Land and home values in St. Louis County, Missouri

St. Louis County (metro core, St. Louis, MO-IL) covers 509 square miles and 236 census tracts. Residential land is about $8.16 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.34 in its most rural tracts to $23.10 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $297,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,340 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in St. Louis County, MO?

Residential land in St. Louis County, MO is about $8.16 per square foot, or $355,580 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $88,895. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in St. Louis County, MO?

The typical (median) home in St. Louis County, MO is worth about $297,000, with entry-level homes around $221,000 and upper-end homes around $388,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in St. Louis County, MO?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,340 a month in St. Louis County, MO, a 1-bedroom about $1,100 and a 3-bedroom about $1,720 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in St. Louis County, MO?

Farmland averages about $13,400 an acre in St. Louis County, MO (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
