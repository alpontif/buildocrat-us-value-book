Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Webb City, MO?

Residential land in Webb City, MO is about $2.29 per square foot, or $99,927 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $24,982. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Webb City, MO?

The typical (median) home in Webb City, MO is worth about $192,000, with entry-level homes around $152,000 and upper-end homes around $286,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Webb City, MO?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,020 a month in Webb City, MO, a 1-bedroom about $820 and a 3-bedroom about $1,330 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
