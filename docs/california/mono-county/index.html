Land and home values in Mono County, California

Mono County (rural) covers 3,049 square miles and 4 census tracts. Residential land is about $43.80 per sq ft at the county median, from $20.50 in its most rural tracts to $43.80 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $782,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,650 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Mono County, CA?

Residential land in Mono County, CA is about $43.80 per square foot, or $1.91M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $476,982. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Mono County, CA?

The typical (median) home in Mono County, CA is worth about $782,000, with entry-level homes around $537,000 and upper-end homes around $1.15M, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Mono County, CA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,650 a month in Mono County, CA, a 1-bedroom about $1,500 and a 3-bedroom about $2,300 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Mono County, CA?

Farmland averages about $3,510 an acre in Mono County, CA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
