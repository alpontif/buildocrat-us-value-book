Land and home values in Nevada County, California

Nevada County (small town, Truckee-Grass Valley, CA) covers 958 square miles and 26 census tracts. Residential land is about $11.30 per sq ft at the county median, from $7.82 in its most rural tracts to $30.20 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $668,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,060 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Nevada County, CA?

Residential land in Nevada County, CA is about $11.30 per square foot, or $492,228 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $123,057. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Nevada County, CA?

The typical (median) home in Nevada County, CA is worth about $668,000, with entry-level homes around $469,000 and upper-end homes around $861,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Nevada County, CA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,060 a month in Nevada County, CA, a 1-bedroom about $1,570 and a 3-bedroom about $2,890 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Nevada County, CA?

Farmland averages about $9,680 an acre in Nevada County, CA (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
