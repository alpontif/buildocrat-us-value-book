Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Mill Valley, CA?

Residential land in Mill Valley, CA is about $127 per square foot, or $5.55M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $1.39M. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Mill Valley, CA?

The typical (median) home in Mill Valley, CA is worth about $2.02M, with entry-level homes around $1.6M and upper-end homes around $2.02M, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Mill Valley, CA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $3,980 a month in Mill Valley, CA, a 1-bedroom about $3,280 and a 3-bedroom about $5,080 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
