Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Coalinga, CA?

Residential land in Coalinga, CA is about $6.40 per square foot, or $278,828 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $69,707. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Coalinga, CA?

The typical (median) home in Coalinga, CA is worth about $281,000, with entry-level homes around $203,000 and upper-end homes around $435,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Coalinga, CA?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,480 a month in Coalinga, CA, a 1-bedroom about $2,060 and a 3-bedroom about $3,340 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
