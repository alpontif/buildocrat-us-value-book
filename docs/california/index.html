Questions people ask

How much is land worth in California?

Residential land in California is about $46.30 per square foot, or $2.02M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $504,207. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in California?

The highest county medians for residential land are San Francisco County ($205 per sq ft), Santa Clara County ($114 per sq ft), San Mateo County ($110 per sq ft), Orange County ($82.10 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in California?

The lowest county medians are Lassen County ($2.47 per sq ft), Modoc County ($2.81 per sq ft), Siskiyou County ($2.97 per sq ft), Inyo County ($3.42 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in California?

The typical (median) home in California is worth about $784,000, with entry-level homes around $601,000 and upper-end homes around $972,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in California?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,760 a month in California, a 1-bedroom about $2,240 and a 3-bedroom about $3,620 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in California?

Farmland averages about $14,100 an acre in California (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
