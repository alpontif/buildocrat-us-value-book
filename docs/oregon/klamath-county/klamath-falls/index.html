Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Klamath Falls, OR?

Residential land in Klamath Falls, OR is about $4.14 per square foot, or $180,513 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $45,128. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Klamath Falls, OR?

The typical (median) home in Klamath Falls, OR is worth about $319,000, with entry-level homes around $182,000 and upper-end homes around $393,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Klamath Falls, OR?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,130 a month in Klamath Falls, OR, a 1-bedroom about $866 and a 3-bedroom about $1,570 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
