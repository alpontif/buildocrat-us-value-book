Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Tigard, OR?

Residential land in Tigard, OR is about $24.60 per square foot, or $1.07M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $267,894. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Tigard, OR?

The typical (median) home in Tigard, OR is worth about $618,000, with entry-level homes around $475,000 and upper-end homes around $696,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Tigard, OR?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,990 a month in Tigard, OR, a 1-bedroom about $1,730 and a 3-bedroom about $2,710 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
