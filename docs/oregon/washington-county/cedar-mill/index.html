Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Cedar Mill, OR?

Residential land in Cedar Mill, OR is about $38.80 per square foot, or $1.69M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $422,532. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Cedar Mill, OR?

The typical (median) home in Cedar Mill, OR is worth about $889,000, with entry-level homes around $711,000 and upper-end homes around $1.04M, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Cedar Mill, OR?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,060 a month in Cedar Mill, OR, a 1-bedroom about $1,800 and a 3-bedroom about $2,810 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
