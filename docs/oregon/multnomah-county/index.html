Land and home values in Multnomah County, Oregon

Multnomah County (metro core, Portland-Vancouver-Hillsboro, OR-WA) covers 431 square miles and 197 census tracts. Residential land is about $28.60 per sq ft at the county median, from $15.10 in its most rural tracts to $53.90 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $559,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,960 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Multnomah County, OR?

Residential land in Multnomah County, OR is about $28.60 per square foot, or $1.25M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $311,454. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Multnomah County, OR?

The typical (median) home in Multnomah County, OR is worth about $559,000, with entry-level homes around $441,000 and upper-end homes around $696,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Multnomah County, OR?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,960 a month in Multnomah County, OR, a 1-bedroom about $1,720 and a 3-bedroom about $2,700 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Multnomah County, OR?

Farmland averages about $11,600 an acre in Multnomah County, OR (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
