Land and home values in Oregon

Across Oregon's 36 counties, residential land is about $16.40 per sq ft at the state median ($714,384 an acre). The typical home is worth about $484,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,770 a month. Largest city: Portland; capital: Salem. Values as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Oregon?

Residential land in Oregon is about $16.40 per square foot, or $714,384 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $178,596. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Oregon?

The highest county medians for residential land are Multnomah County ($28.60 per sq ft), Hood River County ($27.70 per sq ft), Washington County ($24.40 per sq ft), Clackamas County ($20.50 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Oregon?

The lowest county medians are Gilliam County ($1.98 per sq ft), Sherman County ($2.29 per sq ft), Grant County ($2.55 per sq ft), Harney County ($2.67 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Oregon?

The typical (median) home in Oregon is worth about $484,000, with entry-level homes around $378,000 and upper-end homes around $652,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Oregon?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,770 a month in Oregon, a 1-bedroom about $1,480 and a 3-bedroom about $2,440 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Oregon?

Farmland averages about $3,880 an acre in Oregon (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
