Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Tennessee?

Residential land in Tennessee is about $5.00 per square foot, or $218,018 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $54,504. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Tennessee?

The highest county medians for residential land are Moore County ($20.40 per sq ft), Williamson County ($19.30 per sq ft), Meigs County ($15.20 per sq ft), Wilson County ($9.88 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Tennessee?

The lowest county medians are Lake County ($0.68 per sq ft), Perry County ($0.74 per sq ft), Hancock County ($0.93 per sq ft), Clay County ($0.93 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Tennessee?

The typical (median) home in Tennessee is worth about $290,000, with entry-level homes around $200,000 and upper-end homes around $410,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Tennessee?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,320 a month in Tennessee, a 1-bedroom about $1,140 and a 3-bedroom about $1,700 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Tennessee?

Farmland averages about $6,500 an acre in Tennessee (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
