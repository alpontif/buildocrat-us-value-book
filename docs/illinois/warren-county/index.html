Land and home values in Warren County, Illinois

Warren County (rural) covers 542 square miles and 5 census tracts. Residential land is about $0.96 per sq ft at the county median, from $0.71 in its most rural tracts to $1.27 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $128,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $985 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Warren County, IL?

Residential land in Warren County, IL is about $0.96 per square foot, or $41,774 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $10,444. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Warren County, IL?

The typical (median) home in Warren County, IL is worth about $128,000, with entry-level homes around $97,700 and upper-end homes around $191,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Warren County, IL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $985 a month in Warren County, IL, a 1-bedroom about $750 and a 3-bedroom about $1,240 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Warren County, IL?

Farmland averages about $11,600 an acre in Warren County, IL (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
