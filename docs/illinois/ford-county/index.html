Land and home values in Ford County, Illinois

Ford County (suburban, Champaign-Urbana, IL) covers 486 square miles and 5 census tracts. Residential land is about $2.10 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.12 in its most rural tracts to $2.82 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $151,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,020 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Ford County, IL?

Residential land in Ford County, IL is about $2.10 per square foot, or $91,650 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $22,913. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Ford County, IL?

The typical (median) home in Ford County, IL is worth about $151,000, with entry-level homes around $112,000 and upper-end homes around $247,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Ford County, IL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,020 a month in Ford County, IL, a 1-bedroom about $820 and a 3-bedroom about $1,410 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Ford County, IL?

Farmland averages about $11,200 an acre in Ford County, IL (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
