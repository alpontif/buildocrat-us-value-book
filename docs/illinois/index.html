Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Illinois?

Residential land in Illinois is about $7.06 per square foot, or $307,403 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $76,851. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Illinois?

The highest county medians for residential land are Cook County ($16.80 per sq ft), DuPage County ($16.50 per sq ft), Lake County ($8.49 per sq ft), Kane County ($8.07 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Illinois?

The lowest county medians are Alexander County ($0.50 per sq ft), Pulaski County ($0.74 per sq ft), Gallatin County ($0.74 per sq ft), Jasper County ($0.76 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Illinois?

The typical (median) home in Illinois is worth about $302,000, with entry-level homes around $226,000 and upper-end homes around $409,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Illinois?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,570 a month in Illinois, a 1-bedroom about $1,380 and a 3-bedroom about $2,040 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Illinois?

Farmland averages about $9,250 an acre in Illinois (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
