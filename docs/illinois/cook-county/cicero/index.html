Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Cicero, IL?

Residential land in Cicero, IL is about $6.82 per square foot, or $296,949 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $74,237. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Cicero, IL?

The typical (median) home in Cicero, IL is worth about $294,000, with entry-level homes around $225,000 and upper-end homes around $395,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Cicero, IL?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,490 a month in Cicero, IL, a 1-bedroom about $1,320 and a 3-bedroom about $1,920 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
