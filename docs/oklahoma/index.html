Land and home values in Oklahoma

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Oklahoma?

Residential land in Oklahoma is about $3.01 per square foot, or $131,203 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $32,801. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Oklahoma?

The highest county medians for residential land are Delaware County ($6.78 per sq ft), Tulsa County ($5.01 per sq ft), Cleveland County ($4.63 per sq ft), Oklahoma County ($4.38 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Oklahoma?

The lowest county medians are Tillman County ($0.44 per sq ft), Jefferson County ($0.47 per sq ft), Harmon County ($0.47 per sq ft), Okfuskee County ($0.61 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Oklahoma?

The typical (median) home in Oklahoma is worth about $196,000, with entry-level homes around $128,000 and upper-end homes around $283,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Oklahoma?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,130 a month in Oklahoma, a 1-bedroom about $910 and a 3-bedroom about $1,500 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Oklahoma?

Farmland averages about $2,620 an acre in Oklahoma (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
