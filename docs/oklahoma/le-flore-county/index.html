Land and home values in Le Flore County, Oklahoma

Le Flore County (rural) covers 1,589 square miles and 12 census tracts. Residential land is about $1.78 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.35 in its most rural tracts to $2.62 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $158,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $955 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Le Flore County, OK?

Residential land in Le Flore County, OK is about $1.78 per square foot, or $77,667 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $19,417. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Le Flore County, OK?

The typical (median) home in Le Flore County, OK is worth about $158,000, with entry-level homes around $89,800 and upper-end homes around $234,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Le Flore County, OK?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $955 a month in Le Flore County, OK, a 1-bedroom about $760 and a 3-bedroom about $1,320 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Le Flore County, OK?

Farmland averages about $3,670 an acre in Le Flore County, OK (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
