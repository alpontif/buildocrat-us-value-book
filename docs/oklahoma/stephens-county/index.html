Land and home values in Stephens County, Oklahoma

Stephens County (small town, Duncan, OK) covers 870 square miles and 11 census tracts. Residential land is about $2.20 per sq ft at the county median, from $0.87 in its most rural tracts to $3.90 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $177,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $940 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Stephens County, OK?

Residential land in Stephens County, OK is about $2.20 per square foot, or $95,701 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $23,925. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Stephens County, OK?

The typical (median) home in Stephens County, OK is worth about $177,000, with entry-level homes around $96,800 and upper-end homes around $236,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Stephens County, OK?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $940 a month in Stephens County, OK, a 1-bedroom about $780 and a 3-bedroom about $1,300 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Stephens County, OK?

Farmland averages about $3,050 an acre in Stephens County, OK (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
