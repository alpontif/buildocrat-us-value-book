Questions people ask

How much is land worth in New Jersey?

Residential land in New Jersey is about $24.20 per square foot, or $1.05M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $263,538. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in New Jersey?

The highest county medians for residential land are Cape May County ($53.20 per sq ft), Hudson County ($50.20 per sq ft), Bergen County ($41.00 per sq ft), Monmouth County ($30.10 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in New Jersey?

The lowest county medians are Cumberland County ($5.43 per sq ft), Salem County ($5.85 per sq ft), Camden County ($9.29 per sq ft), Gloucester County ($9.48 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in New Jersey?

The typical (median) home in New Jersey is worth about $539,000, with entry-level homes around $414,000 and upper-end homes around $722,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in New Jersey?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,340 a month in New Jersey, a 1-bedroom about $1,940 and a 3-bedroom about $2,900 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in New Jersey?

Farmland averages about $17,000 an acre in New Jersey (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
