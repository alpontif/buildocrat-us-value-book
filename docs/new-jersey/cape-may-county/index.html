Land and home values in Cape May County, New Jersey

Cape May County (metro core, Atlantic City-Hammonton, NJ) covers 252 square miles and 33 census tracts. Residential land is about $53.20 per sq ft at the county median, from $12.00 in its most rural tracts to $318 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $586,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,980 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Cape May County, NJ?

Residential land in Cape May County, NJ is about $53.20 per square foot, or $2.32M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $579,348. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Cape May County, NJ?

The typical (median) home in Cape May County, NJ is worth about $586,000, with entry-level homes around $403,000 and upper-end homes around $960,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Cape May County, NJ?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,980 a month in Cape May County, NJ, a 1-bedroom about $1,510 and a 3-bedroom about $2,660 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Cape May County, NJ?

Farmland averages about $23,100 an acre in Cape May County, NJ (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
