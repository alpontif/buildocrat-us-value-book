Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Wausau, WI?

Residential land in Wausau, WI is about $4.03 per square foot, or $175,677 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $43,919. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Wausau, WI?

The typical (median) home in Wausau, WI is worth about $199,000, with entry-level homes around $138,000 and upper-end homes around $241,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Wausau, WI?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,210 a month in Wausau, WI, a 1-bedroom about $943 and a 3-bedroom about $1,600 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
