Land and home values in Wisconsin

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Wisconsin?

Residential land in Wisconsin is about $6.55 per square foot, or $285,362 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $71,340. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Wisconsin?

The highest county medians for residential land are Menominee County ($28.40 per sq ft), Sawyer County ($21.60 per sq ft), Waushara County ($18.70 per sq ft), Green Lake County ($18.00 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Wisconsin?

The lowest county medians are Richland County ($1.86 per sq ft), Lafayette County ($1.97 per sq ft), Pepin County ($2.11 per sq ft), Marinette County ($2.22 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Wisconsin?

The typical (median) home in Wisconsin is worth about $299,000, with entry-level homes around $212,000 and upper-end homes around $424,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Wisconsin?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,230 a month in Wisconsin, a 1-bedroom about $990 and a 3-bedroom about $1,580 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Wisconsin?

Farmland averages about $6,700 an acre in Wisconsin (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
