Land and home values in Ross County, Ohio

Ross County (small town, Chillicothe, OH) covers 689 square miles and 17 census tracts. Residential land is about $3.23 per sq ft at the county median, from $2.01 in its most rural tracts to $4.83 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $214,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,080 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Ross County, OH?

Residential land in Ross County, OH is about $3.23 per square foot, or $140,481 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $35,120. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Ross County, OH?

The typical (median) home in Ross County, OH is worth about $214,000, with entry-level homes around $140,000 and upper-end homes around $302,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Ross County, OH?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,080 a month in Ross County, OH, a 1-bedroom about $900 and a 3-bedroom about $1,360 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Ross County, OH?

Farmland averages about $8,400 an acre in Ross County, OH (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
