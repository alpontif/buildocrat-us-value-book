Land and home values in Portage County, Ohio

Portage County (metro core, Akron, OH) covers 487 square miles and 38 census tracts. Residential land is about $4.90 per sq ft at the county median, from $2.34 in its most rural tracts to $9.20 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $261,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,250 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Portage County, OH?

Residential land in Portage County, OH is about $4.90 per square foot, or $213,488 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $53,372. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Portage County, OH?

The typical (median) home in Portage County, OH is worth about $261,000, with entry-level homes around $188,000 and upper-end homes around $383,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Portage County, OH?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,250 a month in Portage County, OH, a 1-bedroom about $975 and a 3-bedroom about $1,530 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Portage County, OH?

Farmland averages about $9,730 an acre in Portage County, OH (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
