Land and home values in Hardin County, Ohio

Hardin County (rural) covers 470 square miles and 8 census tracts. Residential land is about $1.63 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.03 in its most rural tracts to $2.05 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $170,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $990 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Hardin County, OH?

Residential land in Hardin County, OH is about $1.63 per square foot, or $70,959 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $17,740. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Hardin County, OH?

The typical (median) home in Hardin County, OH is worth about $170,000, with entry-level homes around $98,500 and upper-end homes around $264,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Hardin County, OH?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $990 a month in Hardin County, OH, a 1-bedroom about $820 and a 3-bedroom about $1,250 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Hardin County, OH?

Farmland averages about $12,000 an acre in Hardin County, OH (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
