Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Ohio?

Residential land in Ohio is about $4.86 per square foot, or $211,876 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $52,969. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Ohio?

The highest county medians for residential land are Ottawa County ($12.40 per sq ft), Delaware County ($11.00 per sq ft), Geauga County ($8.61 per sq ft), Franklin County ($7.76 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Ohio?

The lowest county medians are Harrison County ($1.05 per sq ft), Meigs County ($1.27 per sq ft), Monroe County ($1.35 per sq ft), Vinton County ($1.50 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Ohio?

The typical (median) home in Ohio is worth about $235,000, with entry-level homes around $169,000 and upper-end homes around $324,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Ohio?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,220 a month in Ohio, a 1-bedroom about $975 and a 3-bedroom about $1,540 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Ohio?

Farmland averages about $9,650 an acre in Ohio (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
