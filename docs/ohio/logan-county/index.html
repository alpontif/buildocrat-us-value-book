Land and home values in Logan County, Ohio

Logan County (small town, Bellefontaine, OH) covers 459 square miles and 11 census tracts. Residential land is about $4.82 per sq ft at the county median, from $2.81 in its most rural tracts to $18.50 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $250,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,080 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Logan County, OH?

Residential land in Logan County, OH is about $4.82 per square foot, or $209,959 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $52,490. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Logan County, OH?

The typical (median) home in Logan County, OH is worth about $250,000, with entry-level homes around $164,000 and upper-end homes around $340,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Logan County, OH?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,080 a month in Logan County, OH, a 1-bedroom about $820 and a 3-bedroom about $1,310 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Logan County, OH?

Farmland averages about $11,100 an acre in Logan County, OH (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
