Land and home values in Miami County, Kansas

Miami County (suburban, Kansas City, MO-KS) covers 576 square miles and 8 census tracts. Residential land is about $5.09 per sq ft at the county median, from $2.14 in its most rural tracts to $5.96 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $369,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,260 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Miami County, KS?

Residential land in Miami County, KS is about $5.09 per square foot, or $221,895 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $55,474. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Miami County, KS?

The typical (median) home in Miami County, KS is worth about $369,000, with entry-level homes around $234,000 and upper-end homes around $524,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Miami County, KS?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,260 a month in Miami County, KS, a 1-bedroom about $1,100 and a 3-bedroom about $1,640 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Miami County, KS?

Farmland averages about $5,540 an acre in Miami County, KS (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
