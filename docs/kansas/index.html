Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Kansas?

Residential land in Kansas is about $2.65 per square foot, or $115,565 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $28,891. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Kansas?

The highest county medians for residential land are Johnson County ($9.57 per sq ft), Douglas County ($7.25 per sq ft), Miami County ($5.09 per sq ft), Linn County ($5.04 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Kansas?

The lowest county medians are Jewell County ($0.31 per sq ft), Chautauqua County ($0.32 per sq ft), Stanton County ($0.34 per sq ft), Edwards County ($0.34 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Kansas?

The typical (median) home in Kansas is worth about $215,000, with entry-level homes around $150,000 and upper-end homes around $305,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Kansas?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,120 a month in Kansas, a 1-bedroom about $880 and a 3-bedroom about $1,470 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Kansas?

Farmland averages about $3,200 an acre in Kansas (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
