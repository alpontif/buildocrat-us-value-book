Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Sonterra, TX?

Residential land in Sonterra, TX is about $5.09 per square foot, or $221,633 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $55,408. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Sonterra, TX?

The typical (median) home in Sonterra, TX is worth about $267,000, with entry-level homes around $230,000 and upper-end homes around $366,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Sonterra, TX?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,930 a month in Sonterra, TX, a 1-bedroom about $1,640 and a 3-bedroom about $2,450 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
