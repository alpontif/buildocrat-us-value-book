Land and home values in Williamson County, Texas

Williamson County (metro core, Austin-Round Rock-San Marcos, TX) covers 1,116 square miles and 135 census tracts. Residential land is about $9.37 per sq ft at the county median, from $5.01 in its most rural tracts to $15.30 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $432,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,040 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Williamson County, TX?

Residential land in Williamson County, TX is about $9.37 per square foot, or $408,157 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $102,039. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Williamson County, TX?

The typical (median) home in Williamson County, TX is worth about $432,000, with entry-level homes around $343,000 and upper-end homes around $573,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Williamson County, TX?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,040 a month in Williamson County, TX, a 1-bedroom about $1,720 and a 3-bedroom about $2,600 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Williamson County, TX?

Farmland averages about $6,120 an acre in Williamson County, TX (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
