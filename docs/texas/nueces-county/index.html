Land and home values in Nueces County, Texas

Nueces County (metro core, Corpus Christi, TX) covers 839 square miles and 97 census tracts. Residential land is about $5.37 per sq ft at the county median, from $1.79 in its most rural tracts to $17.60 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $223,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,440 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Nueces County, TX?

Residential land in Nueces County, TX is about $5.37 per square foot, or $233,874 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $58,468. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Nueces County, TX?

The typical (median) home in Nueces County, TX is worth about $223,000, with entry-level homes around $170,000 and upper-end homes around $298,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Nueces County, TX?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,440 a month in Nueces County, TX, a 1-bedroom about $1,170 and a 3-bedroom about $1,880 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Nueces County, TX?

Farmland averages about $3,760 an acre in Nueces County, TX (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
