Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Texas?

Residential land in Texas is about $5.78 per square foot, or $251,777 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $62,944. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Texas?

The highest county medians for residential land are Llano County ($24.00 per sq ft), Travis County ($16.20 per sq ft), Gillespie County ($14.40 per sq ft), Aransas County ($14.00 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Texas?

The lowest county medians are Stonewall County ($0.23 per sq ft), King County ($0.26 per sq ft), Cochran County ($0.27 per sq ft), Cottle County ($0.31 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Texas?

The typical (median) home in Texas is worth about $279,000, with entry-level homes around $207,000 and upper-end homes around $375,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Texas?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,600 a month in Texas, a 1-bedroom about $1,340 and a 3-bedroom about $2,090 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Texas?

Farmland averages about $3,100 an acre in Texas (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
