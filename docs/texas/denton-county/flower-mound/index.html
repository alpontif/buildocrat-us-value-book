Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Flower Mound, TX?

Residential land in Flower Mound, TX is about $16.50 per square foot, or $718,740 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $179,685. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Flower Mound, TX?

The typical (median) home in Flower Mound, TX is worth about $568,000, with entry-level homes around $539,000 and upper-end homes around $876,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Flower Mound, TX?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $3,110 a month in Flower Mound, TX, a 1-bedroom about $2,670 and a 3-bedroom about $3,950 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
