Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Denton, TX?

Residential land in Denton, TX is about $9.29 per square foot, or $404,455 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $101,114. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Denton, TX?

The typical (median) home in Denton, TX is worth about $353,000, with entry-level homes around $205,000 and upper-end homes around $353,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Denton, TX?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,720 a month in Denton, TX, a 1-bedroom about $1,470 and a 3-bedroom about $2,160 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
