Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Universal City, TX?

Residential land in Universal City, TX is about $4.27 per square foot, or $185,827 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $46,457. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Universal City, TX?

The typical (median) home in Universal City, TX is worth about $272,000, with entry-level homes around $195,000 and upper-end homes around $295,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Universal City, TX?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,360 a month in Universal City, TX, a 1-bedroom about $1,110 and a 3-bedroom about $1,730 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
