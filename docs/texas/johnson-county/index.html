Land and home values in Johnson County, Texas

Johnson County (metro core, Dallas-Fort Worth-Arlington, TX) covers 725 square miles and 39 census tracts. Residential land is about $4.44 per sq ft at the county median, from $3.04 in its most rural tracts to $8.24 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $277,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,610 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Johnson County, TX?

Residential land in Johnson County, TX is about $4.44 per square foot, or $193,494 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $48,373. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Johnson County, TX?

The typical (median) home in Johnson County, TX is worth about $277,000, with entry-level homes around $204,000 and upper-end homes around $395,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Johnson County, TX?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,610 a month in Johnson County, TX, a 1-bedroom about $1,380 and a 3-bedroom about $2,120 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Johnson County, TX?

Farmland averages about $7,320 an acre in Johnson County, TX (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
