Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Columbus, NE?

Residential land in Columbus, NE is about $5.54 per square foot, or $241,410 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $60,352. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Columbus, NE?

The typical (median) home in Columbus, NE is worth about $240,000, with entry-level homes around $179,000 and upper-end homes around $318,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Columbus, NE?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,010 a month in Columbus, NE, a 1-bedroom about $772 and a 3-bedroom about $1,330 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
