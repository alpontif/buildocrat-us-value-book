Land and home values in Cass County, Nebraska

Cass County (suburban, Omaha, NE-IA) covers 557 square miles and 6 census tracts. Residential land is about $6.37 per sq ft at the county median, from $4.05 in its most rural tracts to $8.43 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $345,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,240 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Cass County, NE?

Residential land in Cass County, NE is about $6.37 per square foot, or $277,434 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $69,358. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Cass County, NE?

The typical (median) home in Cass County, NE is worth about $345,000, with entry-level homes around $207,000 and upper-end homes around $518,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Cass County, NE?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,240 a month in Cass County, NE, a 1-bedroom about $1,050 and a 3-bedroom about $1,650 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Cass County, NE?

Farmland averages about $8,860 an acre in Cass County, NE (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
