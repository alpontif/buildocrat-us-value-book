Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Nebraska?

Residential land in Nebraska is about $4.54 per square foot, or $197,675 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $49,419. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Nebraska?

The highest county medians for residential land are Gosper County ($15.50 per sq ft), Lancaster County ($7.86 per sq ft), Cass County ($6.37 per sq ft), Dodge County ($5.83 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Nebraska?

The lowest county medians are Pawnee County ($0.43 per sq ft), Webster County ($0.51 per sq ft), Franklin County ($0.52 per sq ft), Hooker County ($0.54 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Nebraska?

The typical (median) home in Nebraska is worth about $246,000, with entry-level homes around $178,000 and upper-end homes around $331,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Nebraska?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,200 a month in Nebraska, a 1-bedroom about $975 and a 3-bedroom about $1,590 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Nebraska?

Farmland averages about $4,400 an acre in Nebraska (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
