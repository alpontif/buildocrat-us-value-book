Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Wyoming?

Residential land in Wyoming is about $7.03 per square foot, or $306,053 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $76,513. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Wyoming?

The highest county medians for residential land are Teton County ($89.30 per sq ft), Park County ($9.38 per sq ft), Albany County ($9.26 per sq ft), Sweetwater County ($9.10 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Wyoming?

The lowest county medians are Niobrara County ($2.40 per sq ft), Weston County ($2.97 per sq ft), Carbon County ($3.20 per sq ft), Goshen County ($3.22 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Wyoming?

The typical (median) home in Wyoming is worth about $337,000, with entry-level homes around $248,000 and upper-end homes around $459,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Wyoming?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,060 a month in Wyoming, a 1-bedroom about $860 and a 3-bedroom about $1,430 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Wyoming?

Farmland averages about $1,030 an acre in Wyoming (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
