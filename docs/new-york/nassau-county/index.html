Land and home values in Nassau County, New York

Nassau County (metro core, New York-Newark-Jersey City, NY-NJ) covers 285 square miles and 286 census tracts. Residential land is about $41.40 per sq ft at the county median, from $26.90 in its most rural tracts to $92.90 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $776,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,910 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Nassau County, NY?

Residential land in Nassau County, NY is about $41.40 per square foot, or $1.8M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $450,846. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Nassau County, NY?

The typical (median) home in Nassau County, NY is worth about $776,000, with entry-level homes around $632,000 and upper-end homes around $955,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Nassau County, NY?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,910 a month in Nassau County, NY, a 1-bedroom about $2,510 and a 3-bedroom about $3,760 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Nassau County, NY?

Farmland averages about $13,500 an acre in Nassau County, NY (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
