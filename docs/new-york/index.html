Questions people ask

How much is land worth in New York?

Residential land in New York is about $34.40 per square foot, or $1.5M an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $374,616. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in New York?

The highest county medians for residential land are New York County ($335 per sq ft), Kings County ($221 per sq ft), Queens County ($87.80 per sq ft), Richmond County ($61.50 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in New York?

The lowest county medians are Chenango County ($1.53 per sq ft), Delaware County ($1.76 per sq ft), St. Lawrence County ($1.86 per sq ft), Chemung County ($1.92 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in New York?

The typical (median) home in New York is worth about $618,000, with entry-level homes around $427,000 and upper-end homes around $816,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in New York?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,570 a month in New York, a 1-bedroom about $2,290 and a 3-bedroom about $3,240 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in New York?

Farmland averages about $4,500 an acre in New York (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
