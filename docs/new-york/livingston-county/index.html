Land and home values in Livingston County, New York

Livingston County (suburban, Rochester, NY) covers 632 square miles and 18 census tracts. Residential land is about $5.89 per sq ft at the county median, from $3.05 in its most rural tracts to $15.60 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $213,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,300 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Livingston County, NY?

Residential land in Livingston County, NY is about $5.89 per square foot, or $256,525 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $64,131. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Livingston County, NY?

The typical (median) home in Livingston County, NY is worth about $213,000, with entry-level homes around $155,000 and upper-end homes around $325,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Livingston County, NY?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,300 a month in Livingston County, NY, a 1-bedroom about $1,050 and a 3-bedroom about $1,560 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Livingston County, NY?

Farmland averages about $5,550 an acre in Livingston County, NY (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
