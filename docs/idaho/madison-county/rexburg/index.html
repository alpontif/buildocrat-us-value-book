Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Rexburg, ID?

Residential land in Rexburg, ID is about $8.02 per square foot, or $349,438 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $87,360. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Rexburg, ID?

The typical (median) home in Rexburg, ID is worth about $432,000, with entry-level homes around $326,000 and upper-end homes around $604,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Rexburg, ID?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,120 a month in Rexburg, ID, a 1-bedroom about $1,020 and a 3-bedroom about $1,560 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).
