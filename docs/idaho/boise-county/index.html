Land and home values in Boise County, Idaho

Boise County (suburban, Boise City, ID) covers 1,899 square miles and 3 census tracts. Residential land is about $9.58 per sq ft at the county median, from $9.58 in its most rural tracts to $9.58 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $549,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,680 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Boise County, ID?

Residential land in Boise County, ID is about $9.58 per square foot, or $417,435 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $104,359. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Boise County, ID?

The typical (median) home in Boise County, ID is worth about $549,000, with entry-level homes around $299,000 and upper-end homes around $826,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Boise County, ID?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,680 a month in Boise County, ID, a 1-bedroom about $1,400 and a 3-bedroom about $2,340 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Boise County, ID?

Farmland averages about $2,440 an acre in Boise County, ID (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
