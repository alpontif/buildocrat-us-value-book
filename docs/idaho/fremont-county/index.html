Land and home values in Fremont County, Idaho

Fremont County (small town, Rexburg, ID) covers 1,864 square miles and 4 census tracts. Residential land is about $6.49 per sq ft at the county median, from $6.27 in its most rural tracts to $13.00 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $370,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,020 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Fremont County, ID?

Residential land in Fremont County, ID is about $6.49 per square foot, or $282,792 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $70,698. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Fremont County, ID?

The typical (median) home in Fremont County, ID is worth about $370,000, with entry-level homes around $211,000 and upper-end homes around $538,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Fremont County, ID?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,020 a month in Fremont County, ID, a 1-bedroom about $930 and a 3-bedroom about $1,420 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Fremont County, ID?

Farmland averages about $5,730 an acre in Fremont County, ID (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
