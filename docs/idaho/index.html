Land and home values in Idaho

Across Idaho's 44 counties, residential land is about $8.98 per sq ft at the state median ($391,343 an acre). The typical home is worth about $453,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,490 a month. Largest city: Boise City; capital: Boise. Values as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Idaho?

Residential land in Idaho is about $8.98 per square foot, or $391,343 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $97,836. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Idaho?

The highest county medians for residential land are Blaine County ($53.70 per sq ft), Valley County ($21.90 per sq ft), Ada County ($17.70 per sq ft), Bonner County ($16.30 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Idaho?

The lowest county medians are Clark County ($1.51 per sq ft), Butte County ($1.51 per sq ft), Lewis County ($1.60 per sq ft), Lincoln County ($1.90 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Idaho?

The typical (median) home in Idaho is worth about $453,000, with entry-level homes around $335,000 and upper-end homes around $605,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Idaho?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,490 a month in Idaho, a 1-bedroom about $1,240 and a 3-bedroom about $2,080 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Idaho?

Farmland averages about $4,750 an acre in Idaho (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
