Land and home values in Douglas County, Nevada

Douglas County (small town, Gardnerville Ranchos, NV-CA) covers 711 square miles and 17 census tracts. Residential land is about $12.40 per sq ft at the county median, from $5.72 in its most rural tracts to $115 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $729,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,620 a month, as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Douglas County, NV?

Residential land in Douglas County, NV is about $12.40 per square foot, or $540,144 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $135,036. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Douglas County, NV?

The typical (median) home in Douglas County, NV is worth about $729,000, with entry-level homes around $563,000 and upper-end homes around $987,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Douglas County, NV?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,620 a month in Douglas County, NV, a 1-bedroom about $1,290 and a 3-bedroom about $2,250 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Douglas County, NV?

Farmland averages about $2,910 an acre in Douglas County, NV (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
