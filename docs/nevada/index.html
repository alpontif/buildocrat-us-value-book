Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Nevada?

Residential land in Nevada is about $12.20 per square foot, or $531,432 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $132,858. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Nevada?

The highest county medians for residential land are Washoe County ($13.30 per sq ft), Clark County ($12.50 per sq ft), Douglas County ($12.40 per sq ft), Carson City ($11.00 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Nevada?

The lowest county medians are Eureka County ($0.55 per sq ft), Esmeralda County ($0.81 per sq ft), Mineral County ($1.49 per sq ft), Pershing County ($1.63 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Nevada?

The typical (median) home in Nevada is worth about $457,000, with entry-level homes around $363,000 and upper-end homes around $546,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Nevada?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,860 a month in Nevada, a 1-bedroom about $1,560 and a 3-bedroom about $2,580 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Nevada?

Farmland averages about $1,230 an acre in Nevada (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
