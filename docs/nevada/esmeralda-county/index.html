Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Esmeralda County, NV?

Residential land in Esmeralda County, NV is about $0.81 per square foot, or $35,109 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $8,777. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Esmeralda County, NV?

The typical (median) home in Esmeralda County, NV is worth about $121,000, with entry-level homes around $43,300 and upper-end homes around $234,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Esmeralda County, NV?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,470 a month in Esmeralda County, NV, a 1-bedroom about $1,120 and a 3-bedroom about $2,060 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Esmeralda County, NV?

Farmland averages about $3,420 an acre in Esmeralda County, NV (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
