Land and home values in Montana

Across Montana's 56 counties, residential land is about $8.84 per sq ft at the state median ($385,158 an acre). The typical home is worth about $418,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $1,520 a month. Largest city: Billings; capital: Helena. Values as of October 19, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Montana?

Residential land in Montana is about $8.84 per square foot, or $385,158 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $96,289. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

Where is land most expensive in Montana?

The highest county medians for residential land are Missoula County ($17.30 per sq ft), Gallatin County ($16.80 per sq ft), Lake County ($15.20 per sq ft), Flathead County ($12.70 per sq ft).

Where is land cheapest in Montana?

The lowest county medians are Wibaux County ($0.62 per sq ft), Roosevelt County ($0.75 per sq ft), Blaine County ($1.01 per sq ft), Sheridan County ($1.06 per sq ft); farmland and remote acreage are cheaper still.

What is the typical home value in Montana?

The typical (median) home in Montana is worth about $418,000, with entry-level homes around $277,000 and upper-end homes around $569,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Montana?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $1,520 a month in Montana, a 1-bedroom about $1,210 and a 3-bedroom about $2,080 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Montana?

Farmland averages about $1,260 an acre in Montana (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
