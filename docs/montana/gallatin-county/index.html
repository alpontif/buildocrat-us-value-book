Land and home values in Gallatin County, Montana

Gallatin County (metro core, Bozeman, MT) covers 2,605 square miles and 26 census tracts. Residential land is about $16.80 per sq ft at the county median, from $9.70 in its most rural tracts to $37.60 in its most valuable. The typical home is worth about $683,000 and a 2-bedroom rents for about $2,220 a month, as of October 12, 2026.

Questions people ask

How much is land worth in Gallatin County, MT?

Residential land in Gallatin County, MT is about $16.80 per square foot, or $731,808 an acre, as of October 2026. A quarter-acre lot (10,890 sq ft) is about $182,952. Smaller lots sell for more per square foot and large tracts for less.

What is the typical home value in Gallatin County, MT?

The typical (median) home in Gallatin County, MT is worth about $683,000, with entry-level homes around $520,000 and upper-end homes around $936,000, as of October 2026.

How much is rent in Gallatin County, MT?

A 2-bedroom rents for about $2,220 a month in Gallatin County, MT, a 1-bedroom about $1,690 and a 3-bedroom about $3,090 (gross rent including utilities, HUD Fair Market Rent basis).

How much is farmland per acre in Gallatin County, MT?

Farmland averages about $3,780 an acre in Gallatin County, MT (USDA, including buildings), well below residential land.
